# Information Management

**Tags:** #information_management #im

## Note
Information Management (IM) is the systematic discipline of collecting, storing, managing, securing, distributing, and eventually archiving or destroying information within an organization. It treats information as a valuable corporate asset.

Key concepts in IM include:
- **Information Lifecycle Management (ILM):** Managing information continuously from creation to destruction.
- **Data Quality & Integrity:** Ensuring information is accurate and reliable.
- **Information Security & Privacy:** Protecting information from unauthorized access.
- **Accessibility & Usability:** Ensuring information is easily retrievable by authorized personnel.

The primary objective of IM is to deliver the *right information* to the *right person* at the *exact right time* in the *right format*.
