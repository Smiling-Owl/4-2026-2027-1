# Information Management vs. Knowledge Management

**Tags:** #information_management #knowledge_management #im_vs_km

## Note
While Information Management (IM) and Knowledge Management (KM) are closely related, they focus on different types of assets:

- **Information Management** focuses on explicit, tangible assets like documents, databases, records, and systems. It deals with the *mechanics* of handling data and information.
- **Knowledge Management (KM)** focuses on intangible assets, such as human expertise, experiences, culture, and skills. It is about fostering an environment where knowledge is naturally shared, created, and applied across the organization.
