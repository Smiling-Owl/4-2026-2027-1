# The IMBOK Framework

**Tags:** #information_management #imbok #framework

## Note
The Information Management Body of Knowledge (IMBOK) is a comprehensive management framework that illustrates how organizations can effectively bridge the gap between technical IT execution and overarching business strategy.

Its primary purpose is to explain how organizations can drive and measure actual business value from their investments in Information Technology. The framework acts as a cycle and relies heavily on alignment; value only flows when its interconnected layers are perfectly aligned, meaning technology implementations must directly correspond to process improvements and strategic goals.
