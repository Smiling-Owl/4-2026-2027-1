# Managerial Issues in IT

**Tags:** #information_management #it #management

## Note
Managers face several issues when dealing with technology:
- **Technology management scope:** Outsourced technology requires senior management to weigh risks and benefits when determining investments in hardware, software, and trained personnel.
- **Managing suppliers:** Cultivate the most useful relationship type with technology suppliers; in most cases, being a good customer sometimes outweighs aggressive supplier treatment.
- **Acquiring technology:** Careful justification and tendering procedures to avoid excessive "just in case" spending.
- **Managing the technology portfolio:** Align current and future technology investments with business strategies and track owned technology and its physical location.
- **Technology competencies:** Maintain internal competencies to track developments, understand IT's nature and origins, and foster experimentation, innovation, and evaluation.
- **Budget management:** Support both centralized capital budgets and local departmental funding.
