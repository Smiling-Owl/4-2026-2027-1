# Information Technology

**Tags:** #information_management #it #technology

## Note
Information Technology (IT) refers to the specific technical components — hardware, software, and communications — that alone may have no useful function and must be engineered into usable systems that serve organizational needs.

The traditional categories of IT are becoming less relevant as devices like smartphones integrate thousands of components into a single unit. IT is now highly commoditized and pre-packaged, so the real, time-consuming effort lies in loading the specific content and essential details required to make a system functional for a business.
