# The DIKW Hierarchy

**Tags:** #information_management #data #knowledge

## Note
The DIKW hierarchy is a fundamental model in information science that illustrates how raw data is transformed into valuable insights. It consists of four distinct layers:
- **Data:** The foundational level of raw, unprocessed facts without context (e.g., the number "42").
- **Information:** Data that has been processed and given context to make it meaningful, answering "who," "what," "where," and "when" (e.g., "42 computers are offline").
- **Knowledge:** The application of human experience and insight to information, enabling an understanding of "how" and "why" things happen (e.g., "42 computers are offline due to a known patch issue").
- **Wisdom:** The highest level, representing the application of knowledge to make sound judgments, solve problems, and predict future outcomes (e.g., "Roll back the update and schedule future patches off-hours").
