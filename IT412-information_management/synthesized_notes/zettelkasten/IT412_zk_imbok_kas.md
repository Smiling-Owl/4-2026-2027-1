# The 5 Knowledge Areas of IMBOK

**Tags:** #information_management #imbok #knowledge_areas

## Note
The IMBOK framework operates as a logical sequence across five critical Knowledge Areas (KAs). Value flows in a cycle: technology enables systems, systems support processes, processes deliver benefits, and benefits fulfill the strategy.

1. **KA 1: Information Technology (IT):** The foundational base layer consisting of technological infrastructure (hardware, software, networks). It exists independently of specific business use.
2. **KA 2: Information Systems (IS):** Raw IT that is packaged, configured, and deployed to serve a specific user need (e.g., a CRM or ERP).
3. **KA 3: Business Processes:** The actual daily workflows and tasks that the IS supports. The focus is purely on *how work gets done*.
4. **KA 4: Business Benefits:** The tangible, measurable advantages gained directly from improving business processes. *IT itself does not deliver business benefits; only improved processes do.*
5. **KA 5: Business Strategy:** The corporate goals and vision. Benefits fulfill this strategy, which in turn dictates what new IT investments (KA 1) are required.
