# Piconets and Scatternets Describe Classical Bluetooth Groups

**Tags:** #data_communications #bluetooth #networks

## Note
In the classical Bluetooth model, a piconet has a primary coordinating up to seven active secondary devices. A scatternet connects piconets through a shared device that participates in multiple groups and can relay between them. This classroom model and its limits do not apply universally across Bluetooth versions or BLE.
