# Guided Transmission Media

**Tags:** #data_communications #transmission_media #guided_media

## Note
Guided media direct and confine signals through a physical link.

- **Twisted pair:** Twisted insulated copper-wire pairs used in telephone networks and LANs.
- **Coaxial cable:** A central conductor protected by insulation, metallic shielding, and an outer jacket; used for cable TV and broadband.
- **Fiber-optic cable:** Glass or plastic fibers that carry light for high-speed, long-distance communication.
- **Stripline:** A conductor between two ground planes, giving high-frequency transmission and EMI immunity.
- **Microstripline:** A conductor separated from a ground plane by a dielectric layer.
