# Multiplexing Trades Simplicity for Capacity

**Tags:** #data_communications #multiplexing #network_design

## Note
Multiplexing can use bandwidth efficiently, increase total data transmission, scale to more signals, and provide flexible channel sharing.

Those benefits add coordination costs: signals may require synchronization, experience latency or degradation, and demand more resource management. Multiplexing is therefore valuable when the gain from sharing a medium outweighs the added system complexity.
