# Network Scope Ranges from Personal to Wide Area

**Tags:** #data_communications #networks #topology

## Note
PAN, LAN, MAN, and WAN classify networks by broad geographic scope: personal devices, a local site, a metropolitan area, and a wide region. Boundaries and example distances are descriptive teaching guides; the specific technology and service determine actual range and capacity.
