# Cellular and Satellite Systems Extend Network Coverage Differently

**Tags:** #data_communications #networks #wireless

## Note
Cellular systems divide coverage into cells served by base stations connected to wider infrastructure. Satellite systems relay signals through space-segment satellites and ground-segment stations and operations centers. GEO provides broad apparent-fixed coverage at higher latency; MEO lies between GEO and LEO, while LEO orbits closer to Earth. Coverage, cost, latency, weather effects, and reliability vary with deployment and service.
