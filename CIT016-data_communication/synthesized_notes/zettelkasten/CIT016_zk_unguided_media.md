# Unguided Transmission Media

**Tags:** #data_communications #transmission_media #wireless

## Note
Unguided media transmit electromagnetic signals without a physical conductor.

- **Radio waves (3 kHz-1 GHz):** Cover broad areas and support radio broadcasting, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth.
- **Microwaves (1-300 GHz):** Support point-to-point and satellite communication but require line of sight.
- **Infrared (300 GHz-400 THz):** Supports short-range links such as remote controls and operates below visible light.

Increasing frequency generally moves these examples from broad-area radio toward directional or short-range communication.
