# Multiplexing Shares a Communication Medium

**Tags:** #data_communications #multiplexing #bandwidth

## Note
Multiplexing combines signals from multiple sources so they can travel through one communication medium or shared bandwidth. The signals are combined before transmission and separated at the receiving end.

Its purpose is to use an available channel more efficiently instead of requiring a completely separate medium for every signal.
