# ENQ/ACK Establishes Permission to Transfer

**Tags:** #data_communications #line_discipline #protocols

## Note
In ENQ/ACK line discipline, a sender enquires whether a receiver is ready before sending data. Acknowledgments and timeouts help coordinate the exchange; EOT indicates that the sender has finished. A timeout can trigger another attempt, while repeated failure ends the session.
