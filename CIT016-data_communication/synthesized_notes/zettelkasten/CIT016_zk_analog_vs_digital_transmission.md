# Analog vs. Digital Transmission

**Tags:** #data_communications #transmission

## Note
Data transmission can be performed in either analog or digital forms:
- **Analog Transmission** sends information in a continuous wave form that is analogous to the original data. Its main advantages are simplicity, high signal fidelity, and real-time processing capabilities.
- **Digital Transmission** sends information in a discrete, binary form (bits of 0s and 1s). Its primary advantages include high accuracy, improved efficiency, and the ability to easily perform multiplexing, compression, and security (encryption).
