# Transmission Media: Guided and Unguided Paths

**Tags:** #data_communications #transmission_media

## Note
A transmission medium is the path that carries data from a transmitter to a receiver.

- **Guided media** confine signals to a physical link such as copper wire, fiber, or a circuit-board conductor.
- **Unguided media** carry electromagnetic signals through free space without a physical conductor.

The central distinction is therefore whether the signal follows a bounded physical path.
