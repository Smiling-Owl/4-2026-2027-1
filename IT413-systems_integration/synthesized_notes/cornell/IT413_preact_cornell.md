## Summary
The Global Medical and Health Tech case study perfectly illustrates that systems integration is fundamentally a socio-technical challenge, not just a technical deployment. Despite Global Medical having superior ERP/PLM systems, the 60-day rapid integration failed severely at the operational level due to a lack of proper IT due diligence, poor communication (overwhelming emails), and inadequate training. When warehouse employees couldn't use the mandatory new ERP system or rely on old manual workarounds, manufacturing ground to a halt. The failure underscores that leadership must actively investigate ground-level workflows rather than dismissing friction as simple resistance to change. A system is only as effective as the users trained to operate it.
