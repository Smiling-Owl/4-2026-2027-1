# Enterprise Integration

**Tags:** #systems_integration #enterprise_architecture #business_strategy

## Note
Enterprise Integration goes beyond basic system integration by adopting a strategic, business-driven approach to achieve interoperability between IT applications, both internal and external to the enterprise. It coordinates cross-functional business processes and involves multiple stakeholders, focusing on a long-term strategic view rather than a localized tactical one.
