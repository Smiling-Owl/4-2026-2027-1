# Ground-Level Workflows vs. Executive Assumptions

**Tags:** #workflows #execution #leadership #problem-solving

## Note
When key performance metrics—such as manufacturing efficiency—drop unexpectedly during a systems integration, executives must investigate the actual, ground-level workflows rather than making assumptions. Friction is often mischaracterized as mere "employee resistance to change" or "insubordination." This blame masks deep, architectural process failures, such as removing essential manual workarounds without providing viable system alternatives, which ultimately causes bottlenecks and halts production.
