# Drivers Explain Why Systems Integration Is Needed

**Tags:** #systems_integration #integration_drivers #business_needs

## Note
A systems integration driver is a business need or organizational pressure that creates a reason to connect systems, share data, or redesign workflows.

Common warning signs are duplicate entry, duplicated or inconsistent information, slow cross-system reporting, batch uploads, disconnected applications, and unmet demand for real-time data. These symptoms identify the need; the driver identifies the desired outcome or pressure behind the work.
