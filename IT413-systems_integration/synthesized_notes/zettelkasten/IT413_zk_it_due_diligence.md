# The Criticality of IT Due Diligence in M&A

**Tags:** #due-diligence #mergers-and-acquisitions #it-leadership #planning

## Note
During the planning phase of systems integration, particularly in Mergers and Acquisitions (M&A), technical due diligence should never be skipped. Assuming that existing systems are "fine" or relying solely on high-level, emailed summaries leads to significant operational blind spots. To ensure a successful integration, IT leadership must be physically and strategically involved from day one, conducting proper site visits to fully audit the existing IT landscape.
