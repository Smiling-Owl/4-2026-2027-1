# Organizational Drivers Create Broad Integration Pressure

**Tags:** #systems_integration #organizational_drivers #organizational_change

## Note
Organizational drivers are external or structural forces that compel integration beyond a single technical project:

- **Industry regulation:** Requires compliance through standardized exchange, security, privacy, or reporting.
- **Globalization:** Extends processes and workflows across organizational and regional units.
- **Consolidation, mergers, and acquisitions:** Requires duplicate or overlapping systems to be combined.
- **Industry deregulation:** Can replace monolithic systems with smaller, manageable, loosely integrated systems.

Unlike project drivers, these pressures originate from the organization's operating environment or structure and may generate several integration projects.
