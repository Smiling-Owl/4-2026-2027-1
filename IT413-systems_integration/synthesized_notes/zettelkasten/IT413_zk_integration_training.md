# Change Management and Training in Systems Implementation

**Tags:** #change-management #training #communication #implementation

## Note
During the implementation phase of systems integration, effective change management and communication are just as critical as technical data mapping. Inadequate training (e.g., confusing sessions, disengaging instructors) leads to user anxiety and inability to use the new system. Furthermore, communication channels must be carefully managed; flooding employees with generic corporate emails can cause them to miss critical notices regarding system training and transition.
