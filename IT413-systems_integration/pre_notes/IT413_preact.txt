Expert Thoughts and Conclusions
What are your thoughts on Systems Integration after reading this case study?
Systems integration is a socio-technical challenge, not just a hardware or software deployment. This case study highlights that forcing a complex system (like an Enterprise Resource Planning tool) onto a workforce without adequate training, communication, and process alignment will paralyze physical operations. A technically superior system is useless if the end-users cannot operate it to perform basic business functions.

What can you conclude about planning, implementing/executing systems integration for an organization or enterprise?

Planning: Never skip technical due diligence. Assuming existing systems are "fine" or relying on high-level summaries leads to blind spots. IT leadership must be physically and strategically involved from day one.

Implementing: Change management and communication are just as critical as data mapping. Flooding employees with generic emails ensures critical training notices get lost.

Executing: When metrics (like manufacturing efficiency) drop during an integration, executives must investigate workflows on the ground. Blaming the drop on "insubordination" or "resistance to change" often masks deep, architectural process failures.
