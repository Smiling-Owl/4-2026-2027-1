# CRISP-DM Framework Overview

## Overview
* **CRISP-DM:** Stands for Cross Industry Standard Process for Data Mining.
* **Definition:** A process model that serves as the base methodology for data science projects.
* **Purpose:** Provides descriptions of project phases, task details, and the relationships between tasks.

## The 6-Step Process

### 1. Business / Project Understanding
* **Focus:** Identifies project objectives and scope.
* **Key Task:** Stakeholders define the core question or problem that data mining needs to address.

### 2. Data Understanding
* **Focus:** Data collection based on business needs.
* **Key Task:** Relevant data is gathered from one or multiple sources once the business goal is clarified.

### 3. Data Preparation
* **Focus:** Building the final dataset for modeling.
* **Key Task:** Stakeholders identify the necessary dimensions and variables to construct the final dataset.

### 4. Modelling
* **Focus:** Applying analytical and algorithmic techniques.
* **Key Task:** Analysts select suitable modeling approaches, such as clustering, predictive modeling, classification, estimation, or a combination.

### 5. Evaluation
* **Focus:** Validating model accuracy and business fit.
* **Key Task:** Analysts test the models to measure success in answering the initial business question and ensure project alignment.

### 6. Deployment
* **Focus:** Utilizing model results for business value.
* **Key Task:** Model outputs are integrated into internal systems, shared with customers, or structured into reports for stakeholders.
