# Data Mining Overview

## 1. What is Data Mining?
* **Definition:** Data mining is the analytical process of sorting through large datasets to identify underlying patterns and relationships.
* **Objective:** Utilizes identified patterns to solve specific business problems through structured data analysis.

## 2. Industry Applications and Uses
* **Telecommunications and Media:** Analyzes customer data to predict behavior and deliver targeted advertising and campaigns.
* **Insurance:** Creates complex models for detecting fraudulent claims, managing risk, and ensuring customer compliance.
* **Finance:** Evaluates market risks and maximizes stock market returns.
* **Retail:** Uses joint purchasing patterns to identify product associations and optimize item placement in aisles and on shelves.
* **Healthcare:** Helps doctors create more accurate diagnoses using patient medical history, physical examination results, medications, and treatment patterns.
* **Manufacturing:** Implements just-in-time fulfillment by predicting when supplies should be reordered or when equipment is likely to fail.

## 3. Common Data Mining Techniques
* **Classification:** Assigns data points from large datasets into categories based on usage.
* **Clustering:** Groups a series of different data points based on shared characteristics.
* **Regression:** Identifies and analyzes relationships between variables to evaluate how one factor affects another.
* **Prediction:** Examines datasets to find patterns and trends, calculating the probability of future outcomes.
* **Association Rules:** Discovers correlations or specific associations between different points in a dataset.
