# CRISP-DM 6-Step Process

**Tags:** #CRISPDM #ProjectLifecycle #DataMining

## Note
The CRISP-DM framework is divided into six sequential phases:
1. **Business Understanding:** Defining the core problem and project objectives.
2. **Data Understanding:** Collecting data relevant to the business goal.
3. **Data Preparation:** Constructing the final dataset (dimensions and variables).
4. **Modelling:** Applying algorithmic approaches like clustering or classification.
5. **Evaluation:** Testing models for accuracy and alignment with business goals.
6. **Deployment:** Integrating the model outputs into business systems or reports.
