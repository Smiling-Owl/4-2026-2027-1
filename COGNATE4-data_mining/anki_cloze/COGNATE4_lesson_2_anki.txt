{{c1::CRISP-DM}} stands for {{c2::Cross Industry Standard Process for Data Mining}}.
{{c1::Cross Industry Standard Process for Data Mining}} is abbreviated as {{c2::CRISP-DM}}.
{{c1::CRISP-DM}} is the {{c2::base methodology and process model}} for data science projects.
The six phases of the CRISP-DM framework are: {{c1::Business Understanding}} (identifying {{c2::objectives and scope}} and defining the {{c3::core problem}}), {{c4::Data Understanding}} ({{c5::gathering relevant data}} from multiple sources), {{c6::Data Preparation}} (building the {{c7::final dataset}} for modeling and identifying {{c8::dimensions and variables}}), {{c9::Modelling}} (applying {{c10::analytical and algorithmic techniques (e.g., clustering, classification)}}), {{c11::Evaluation}} (validating {{c12::model accuracy and business fit}}), and {{c13::Deployment}} (utilizing model results for {{c14::business value}} and integrating outputs into {{c15::systems or stakeholder reports}}).
