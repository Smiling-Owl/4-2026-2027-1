{{c1::Data mining}} is the analytical process of sorting {{c2::large datasets}} to find {{c3::patterns}}.
The objective of data mining is to {{c1::solve business problems using structured data analysis}}.
Applications of data mining include Telecom/Media ({{c1::targeted ads}} and {{c2::customer behavior prediction}}), Insurance ({{c3::fraud detection}}, {{c4::risk management}}, and {{c5::compliance}}), Finance ({{c6::market risk evaluation}} and {{c7::return maximization}}), Retail ({{c8::product association (joint purchasing)}} and {{c9::optimized placement}}), Healthcare ({{c10::accurate diagnoses}} via patient history), and Manufacturing ({{c11::just-in-time fulfillment}} and {{c12::predicting equipment failure}}).
The key techniques used in data mining include {{c1::Classification}} ({{c2::assigning data points into categories}}), {{c3::Clustering}} ({{c4::grouping based on shared characteristics}}), {{c5::Regression}} ({{c6::analyzing relationships between variables}}), {{c7::Prediction}} (finding patterns to {{c8::calculate the probability of future outcomes}}), and {{c9::Association Rules}} ({{c10::discovering correlations between data points}}).
